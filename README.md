A houseplant's one-star review of its human owner, in one line under 280 characters.

> One star. He waters me on his gym schedule — keen in January, gone by February — and my leaves are the cancellation letter he never bothered to send.

`main.ts` writes the line to `entry.txt` in the output directory.
